FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer? Email us directly

How do I place an order on Festivo?

You can browse our products at products page, add items to your cart, and complete checkout securely online. We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, NetBanking, and Cash on Delivery for eligible pin codes. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order.

What are your delivery timelines?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across India. For orders placed before 2 PM Monday–Friday, we offer same-day dispatch. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and other major cities at an additional charge. Free delivery applies on orders above ₹999.

What is your return and refund policy?

We offer hassle-free returns within 7 days of delivery for unused, unopened products in their original packaging. Customised or personalised items are non-returnable unless damaged. Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Do you offer bulk order discounts?

Yes! We offer tiered discounts for bulk orders — 10% off for 50+ items, 15% for 100+ items, and custom pricing for orders above 500 items. Our dedicated bulk orders team will work with you to create a custom quote. Visit our bulk orders page or call us directly for faster assistance.

Can I request a custom decoration theme?

Absolutely! We love creating custom themes. Whether you have a specific colour palette, character theme, or a completely unique concept in mind, our design team can help bring it to life. Send us your requirements via this contact form or WhatsApp us with reference images, and we'll provide a custom quote within 24 hours.
