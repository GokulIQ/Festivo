Our Story & Mission

Six years of making celebrations magical across India.

Our Story

Born from a Love of Celebrations

In 2018, childhood friends Priya Sharma and Rahul Mehta were frustrated by the lack of beautiful, affordable party decoration options in India. Every birthday, wedding, and baby shower meant either spending a fortune on event planners or settling for uninspired, generic setups.

So they decided to change that. Starting from a small 200-square-foot store in Mumbai, Priya and Rahul hand-picked every product — balloons, backdrops, DIY kits, and decor accessories — focusing on quality, colour accuracy, and affordability.

Word spread fast. Within a year, Festivo had served over 1,000 happy families. Today, we operate across 50+ cities, have decorated over 10,000 celebrations, and are proud to be India's most trusted party supplies brand.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make every celebration in India beautiful, memorable, and accessible — by providing premium party supplies, professional decoration services, and creative tools at prices that make sense for every family.

Our Vision

To become South Asia's most loved celebration brand — a name synonymous with joy, creativity, and quality that families, event planners, and corporate teams reach for whenever they want to create something special.
