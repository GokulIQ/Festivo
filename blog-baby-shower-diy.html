DIY Baby Shower Decorations
Under ₹1,000

Priya Sharma February 1, 2024 5 min read 22 comments
👶

Throwing a beautiful baby shower doesn't have to cost a fortune. With a little creativity, some basic craft supplies, and Festivo's affordable decoration range, you can create a stunning baby shower setup for under ₹1,000 that will genuinely impress every guest — and create memories that the parents-to-be will treasure forever.

1. Paper Pompom Backdrop

Tissue paper pompoms are the most budget-friendly DIY decoration available — and they look absolutely stunning when clustered together as a backdrop. A pack of 10 tissue paper sheets costs around ₹50–₹80, and each sheet makes one pompom. Use pastel colours — baby pink, powder blue, mint, lilac, and cream — to create a soft, dreamy backdrop.

Accordion-fold each sheet, tie in the centre with string, and gently separate the layers outward. Hang 12–15 pompoms of mixed sizes from a curtain rod or frame at your backdrop wall for a spectacular effect.

  • Mix sizes: 8-inch and 12-inch pompoms for visual variety
  • Hang at different heights for a cascading, organic look
  • Interweave with fairy lights for an evening party
  • Total cost for 15 pompoms: approx. ₹150

2. DIY Balloon Garland

A pastel balloon garland adds instant impact to any baby shower. Use 5-inch and 11-inch balloons in three to four coordinating pastel shades. A pack of 100 assorted pastel balloons costs around ₹180–₹250, and a balloon decorating strip (₹80) makes the assembly fast and professional-looking.

Arrange the garland along the mantelpiece, across a dining table, or above the gift table. Add a few oversized 24-inch balloons as focal points and finish with a letter balloon spelling "BABY" for a personal touch.

3. Baby Photo Banner

Ask the parents-to-be for a few cherished childhood photos and turn them into a charming photo banner. Print 4×6-inch photos (₹8 each at a local print shop), punch a hole at the top of each one, and string them onto twine with small pegs or washi tape. Hang across the mantelpiece or above the gift table.

This is a deeply personal decoration that will move guests — and the parents will want to keep it forever.

4. Nappy Cake Centrepiece

A "nappy cake" — a tiered display constructed from rolled-up nappies — makes an adorable, practical centrepiece that also doubles as a gift! Roll 30–40 newborn nappies into cylinders, secure with rubber bands, and arrange into a three-tiered circular tower. Wrap each tier with a ribbon and decorate the top with a small stuffed animal, flowers, or a "It's a Boy/Girl!" sign.

5. Themed Table Setup

A cohesive table setup ties the whole party together. Choose a colour palette and maintain it across your tablecloth, napkins, cups, and plates. Budget-friendly options: use pastel-coloured craft paper as a table runner (₹40 per roll), fold paper napkins into fans or floral shapes, and add small vases with affordable artificial florals.

  • Tablecloth: ₹80–₹120
  • Paper plates & cups (set of 20): ₹120
  • Printed napkins: ₹60
  • Small flower vases (3 pcs): ₹150

6. Shopping List & Budget Breakdown

Here's a complete shopping list to achieve a beautiful baby shower décor for under ₹1,000:

  • Tissue paper (5 packs, assorted pastels): ₹250
  • Pastel balloon pack (100 pcs): ₹200
  • Balloon decorating strip: ₹80
  • Twine & mini pegs for photo banner: ₹60
  • Photo prints (10 photos): ₹80
  • Tablecloth & napkins: ₹180
  • Ribbon & washi tape: ₹80
  • Total: ₹930

Final Tips

The key to a beautiful budget baby shower is a consistent colour palette, a few well-placed statement pieces (like the balloon garland and pompom backdrop), and personal touches like the photo banner and nappy cake. You don't need to spend thousands — you need a clear vision and a couple of free afternoons.
