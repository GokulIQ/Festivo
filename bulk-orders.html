Got Questions?

Bulk Order FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity for bulk pricing?
Bulk pricing kicks in at 50 units for most product categories. Some products like backdrops have a minimum of 5 units. Our account manager will clarify exact thresholds in your quote.
How much can I save on bulk orders?
Discounts are tiered: 10% off for 50–100 units, 20% off for 100–500 units, 30% off for 500–1000 units, and up to 40% off for orders above 1000 units. Volume discounts are applied automatically in your custom quote.
Can I get custom packaging or branding?
Yes! Custom packaging is available for orders of 100+ units. You can add your company logo, event branding, or custom labels. Additional lead time of 3–5 business days applies for custom packaging.
What is the typical delivery timeline for bulk orders?
Standard bulk orders ship within 3–5 business days from order confirmation. For custom packaging or large quantities (1000+ units), allow 7–10 business days. We always try to deliver 2 days before your event date.
Do you offer a dedicated account manager?
Every bulk client gets a dedicated account manager who will guide you through product selection, customisation, and logistics. They're available via phone, email, and WhatsApp for the duration of your order.
