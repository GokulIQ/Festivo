Pricing FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden charges?
No hidden charges at all. The price you see is the final price. Delivery is included in all packages. Any optional add-ons will be quoted separately before checkout.
Can I upgrade my package later?
Yes! You can upgrade your package up to 48 hours before your event date. Contact our support team and they'll help you add the extra services or products at the differential price.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 5+ days before the event receive a full refund. Cancellations 2–5 days before receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable. Custom-designed items are non-refundable once production starts.
Do packages include on-site setup?
The Basic package is a DIY delivery-only package. Premium and Luxury packages include on-site setup by our trained decorators. For a standalone setup service, see our Service Packages tab above.
Do you offer discounts for early bookings?
Yes! Book 30+ days in advance and receive 10% off any package. Book 60+ days in advance for 15% off. Early-bird discounts do not stack with other offers but apply automatically at checkout.
